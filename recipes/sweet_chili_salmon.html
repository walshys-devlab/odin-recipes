Sweet Chili Salmon

Sweet Thai chili sauce transforms salmon fillets with the perfect balance of sweet heat, bright lime, garlic, and savory umami. Ready in less than an hour, it's an easy way to add a flavor boost to your usual salmon dinner.

Ingredients

  • 7 tablespoons water, divided
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 1 to 1 1/2 teaspoons sambal oelek
  • 1 large clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 4 (4 ounce) salmon fillets
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • black sesame seeds for garnish (optional)
